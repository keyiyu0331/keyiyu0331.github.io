EXP-012026Stanford · team of 3

Do newer language models think more like brains?

Question
How does layer-by-layer alignment with human brain activity differ across language model generations?
Method
Extracted hidden states from GPT-2 XL and DeepSeek-R1 8B / 14B and predicted fMRI responses (LeBel 2023) with noise-ceiling-normalized ridge regression, comparing peak predictivity, peak depth and area under the layer-wise profile.
Result
DeepSeek models peak in intermediate layers with significantly larger AUC; GPT-2 XL peaks at its final layer. No gain from 8B to 14B, so training paradigm mattered more than scale.
Stack
PyTorch · Hugging Face · ridge regression (SVD) · fMRI
EXP-022025Stanford · team of 2

EXP-032023–25Rangel Lab · UC San Diego

Dentate gyrus rhythms in learning and recall

Question
How do the layers of the hippocampal dentate gyrus coordinate when similar experiences must be linked to different outcomes?
Method
In vivo laminar-probe recordings across the granule cell and hilar layers while rats learned and recalled a spatial reward task. Wrote code for cross-layer coherence and analyzed behavior with MATLAB, SLEAP and DeepSqueak.
Result
Both layers were co-recruited into 20–40 Hz rhythms during path selection and outcome. Incorrect recall choices followed reduced 20–40 Hz activity during learning.
Output
Co-authored SfN 2025 abstract; presented at UCSD URC 2024 and Lab Expo 2025.
EXP-042024Scripps Research

Does STAT3 drive liver cell senescence?

Question
Earlier STAT3 knockout work suggested a role in liver aging. Does overexpressing STAT3 in young mice accelerate senescence?
Method
Delivered STAT3 with lentiviral vectors (EF1A promoter) and measured senescence with SA-β-gal staining on 16 µm and 30 µm liver slices.
Result
Significantly higher senescence in STAT3-overexpressing mice (p ≤ 0.001). Slice thickness affected staining intensity, so thickness must be held constant in comparisons.
Stack
Lentivirus · CRISPR-Cas9 · SA-β-gal staining · ImageJ
